GodCiv

You are the god of a tiny voxel village. You never control a villager directly. Each one has traits, skills, hunger, and fear, and decides for themselves whether to farm, mine, fish, hunt, build, or haul.

Your power is nudge and catastrophe: bless, whisper a directive through the leader, call drought, flood, fire, plague, or quake. Some villagers will disobey. Watch what they actually do.

Drag to look around · pinch to zoom · tap a villager to inspect them · tap the ground for cell info. Works offline after the first load. Progress saves on this device.

A port of the Luanti GodCiv mod. The sim owns the truth; a local AI model, if enabled, only advises.

Settings · Ollama council

The council is advisory only. The model suggests a goal and a focus job; villagers can still disobey. Off by default.

Ollama needs CORS for browser calls from another device. If the test fails from your phone, run Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* on the host, or keep the council off — the game runs fully on its utility brain.
